Discovery call

15 minutes, free

We talk through your question, your data and your deadline. If I'm not the right fit, I'll say so and point you somewhere better.

  • What decision will this analysis support?
  • What data exists, and who owns it?
  • What's the real deadline?
You get: a clear yes or no, and a scope
2

Data audit

Clean before you compute

Most problems hide in the data, not the model. I check it before anything gets fitted.

  • Data dictionary, types and ranges
  • Missing data patterns and duplicates
  • Leakage checks: nothing from the future sneaks in
You get: a data quality report
3

Analysis plan

Lock it before looking

Outcomes, models and sensitivity analyses are written down before results exist. That's what makes the results believable.

  • Primary and secondary outcomes defined
  • Sample size and power, when they apply
  • Reporting guideline chosen up front
You get: a statistical analysis plan (SAP)
4

Analysis

The heavy lifting

Regression, survival, mixed models or machine learning, whichever answers the question. Never the fanciest by default.

  • Internal validation and calibration for models
  • Uncertainty shown, not hidden
  • Sensitivity analyses to stress-test findings
You get: results with honest uncertainty
5

Reporting

Words, tables, figures

Results written the way journals and funders expect, and explained so your whole team can act on them.

  • Publication-ready tables and figures
  • Methods and results text
  • CONSORT, STROBE, TRIPOD+AI or SRQR checklist
You get: a report ready for your manuscript or grant

Handoff

Yours to keep

Everything is scripted and documented, so you can rerun it on new data without calling me.

  • Commented R or Python code
  • A reproducible pipeline
  • A walkthrough session with your team
